What this tests

A two-mode interference mechanism can generate stripes. Removing one mode suppresses interference. A synthetic phase-locking term can concentrate the relative phases. A deliberately seeded vortex checks the topological winding detector.

It does not reproduce the article's exciton reservoir kinetics, measured detuning-vs-voltage, spinor dispersion, relaxation parameters, disorder calibration or optical readout.
