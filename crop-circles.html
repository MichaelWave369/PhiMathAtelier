An offline, reproducible workspace for testing geometric interpretations. Start with the published 2008 Barbury Castle angular sequence, or use your own image and trace its boundaries. No images or data are uploaded anywhere.

01 / Input evidence

Reading protocol

The ten published angle values were measured to roughly 5° precision. This tool divides each interval by the chosen base angle and rounds to the nearest integer. A decimal point after the first digit is a convention, not inferred from interval lengths.

Research safeguard: the original π reading was identified before this program existed. Reproducing it is a verification of the published arithmetic, not a blind discovery or evidence of nonhuman origin.

Possible follow-ups

Independent image measurements, geometry similarity, blinded hypotheses, and sky/geographic correlation with proper multiple-testing controls are future adapters, not claims made by this build.

Automatic circle detection · r0.2

Classical computer vision computes Sobel edges and tests concentric radial structure. It proposes candidate rings, not encoded messages. An optional manual center click constrains the search.

With an image: click the centre, then click successive radial boundary markers in reading order. Eleven boundary clicks produce ten angles. Use known direction and preserve the original image.

Perspective distortion, the centre estimate, boundary ambiguity, and tracing order can change results. This tool does not claim automatic computer vision.
